Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check character count without spaces?
Our tool automatically calculates character count with and without spaces in real time. Simply paste your text into the box above to see both totals listed under the core metrics grid.

How many characters is a 500 word essay?
A typical 500-word essay contains approximately 2,500 to 3,000 characters including spaces, depending on word choice and sentence structure.

What is the maximum Instagram bio character limit?
Instagram limits user bios to exactly 150 characters. Use our built-in progress bar above to ensure your bio stays within the maximum allowance.

How to calculate speech duration from word count?
Speech length is estimated using an average speaking pace of 130 words per minute. For example, a 650-word speech takes approximately 5 minutes to deliver at a standard cadence.

Why is my character count different on Twitter vs Word?
Microsoft Word counts hidden spacing, line breaks, and emojis differently than social media platforms. Our tool matches the character metrics used by modern web platforms like X (Twitter) and LinkedIn.
